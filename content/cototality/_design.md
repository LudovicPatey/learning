# Design log: Cototality, measure and topology

Not rendered. See "Designing tracks and courses" in `AGENTS.md`.

## Objectives
Fifth course of the track "Enumeration degrees". Cototality from three points of view:
combinatorial (graph-cototal, weakly cototal; Σ⁰₂ ⇒ graph-cototal and universality of maximal
independent sets, both proved; simple groups as an exercise), measure (autoreducibility, null
class, weakly 2-random not cototal, Ω cototal, all proved), topology (graph-cototal = points
of a power of the cofinite topology, proved; the zoo and the characterization of cototality
by G_δ spaces stated).

## Sources
Andrews et al. (2019), read in full text (Sections 1–2); Ang Li (arXiv 2402.08247), read in
full text (Sections 1–3); Kihara–Ng–Pauly (arXiv 1904.04107), read in full text (Sections 1,
3.1–3.3); Jacobsen-Grocott's thesis, known through its abstract and talk slides only (the
thesis server blocked automated access).

## Choices
- The measure result is proved by flipping one bit beyond the density string, which needs
  only relative measure greater than 1/2; Li splits the class according to the predicted
  bit. Both are the same idea.
- First drafts of three exercises were muddled (meagerness of the class of cototal sets, total
  sets of measure zero, and a garbled statement) and were rewritten before verification: the
  category result is proved for 1-generic sets directly.
- In the proof of universality of maximal independent sets, the roles of the two fixed
  elements (one in A, one in its complement) were first swapped; corrected against the paper.
