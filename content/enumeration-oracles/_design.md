# Design log: Enumeration oracles

Not rendered. See "Designing tracks and courses" in `AGENTS.md`.

## Objectives
Fourth course of the track "Enumeration degrees". Relativization to enumeration oracles:
Π⁰₁⟨A⟩ classes, ⟨A⟩-generic sets and strong quasi-minimal covers (proved), the relation
PA relative to an enumeration oracle, total degrees PA bounded (proved), continuous degrees
not ⟨self⟩-PA (proved from the codable characterization, admitted), ⟨self⟩-PA degrees (Miller–
Soskova, admitted), PA bounded = continuous (GKMS, admitted), low for PA, universal classes.

## Sources
Goh, Kalimullin, Miller, Soskova, *PA relative to an enumeration oracle* (JSL, online 2022),
read in full text for definitions and statements; Andrews–Igusa–Miller–Soskova (2019);
Ganchev–Kalimullin–Miller–Soskova (dichotomy).

## Choices
The research theorems are stated; the proofs given are those that fit in a lesson and
show the method (relativizing the quasi-minimality argument; reducing to Kreisel's theorem
through codability).
